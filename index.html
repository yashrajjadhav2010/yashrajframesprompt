Terms of Service, Privacy Policy & Official Studio Support

            

          


          

            
            
            
          

        


        

          
          

            
Effective November 2026 · Yashraj Frames AI Studio


            

              
1. Studio Membership & Access Validity

              
Access to full AI portrait prompt instructions, one-click copying, and AI Auto Take launches requires a signed-in Clerk account manually approved by Studio Admin. Approved accounts may carry a time-limited validity period (such as 7-Day Trial, 30 Days, 90 Days, 1 Year, or custom date) or Lifetime access. Access automatically locks when a validity period expires until renewed.

            


            

              
2. Prompt & Creative Asset License

              
All curated style presets, frame previews, and prompt formulas are licensed to authorized Yashraj Frames subscribers for studio portrait production and client framing consultations. Automated scraping, bulk redistribution, or reselling raw prompt catalogs without written permission is prohibited.

            


            

              
3. Custom Framing & Workshop Orders

              
Virtual Framing Studio measurements, print media previews, and pricing estimates act as client visualization proofs. Final delivery timelines, advance deposits, and balance dues generated on job slips or QR receipts are subject to workshop verification prior to printing and framing.

            


            

              
4. Third-Party AI Platforms

              
AI Auto Take opens external platforms (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Bing Image Creator, Grok, Perplexity, or Midjourney) at your discretion. Generated artwork quality, content moderation, and uptime on those services are governed by their respective terms.

            

          


          
          

            
Privacy Commitment · Yashraj Frames AI Studio


            

              
1. Account & Authentication Information

              
When you sign in via Clerk, we store your account identifier, display name, and primary email address strictly to verify studio access, manage subscription tiers and expiry dates, and protect curated prompt libraries.

            


            

              
2. Cloud Sync & Usage Records

              
Approved subscriber preferences, saved favorites, workshop orders, and prompt copy or AI redirect events are synced securely via Supabase so your studio workflow stays synchronized across desktop and mobile devices. We never sell or rent user email addresses or subscriber lists.

            


            

              
3. Client Photos & Virtual Framing

              
Customer photographs loaded into the Virtual Framing Studio are rendered directly inside your browser for live wall previews and client proof downloads. When generating a digital QR receipt, only order specifications and your configured shop contact fields are encoded.

            


            

              
4. Data Requests & Account Deletion

              
You may manage your profile directly through Clerk or email tech.yashrajframes@gmail.com to request data export, correction, or removal of your cloud studio records.
